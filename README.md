## About Me

I am an enterprise analytics and technology leader with more than 15 years of experience across Walmart and Tyson Foods. My background combines strategic leadership, hands-on technical expertise, and a strong understanding of how data can improve business operations and decision-making.

Throughout my career, I have built analytics functions, developed and mentored teams, established KPI frameworks, strengthened data governance, and partnered with business and technology leaders to deliver scalable analytical solutions.

**Selected professional accomplishments include:**

- **Enterprise People Analytics:** Founded and led the Reporting, Data & Analytics function for Walmart U.S. Learning within Global People Analytics, supporting more than one million associates and approximately 200 academies. Hired, coached, and managed four analytics managers.
- **Data Quality and Governance:** Established KPI definitions, reporting standards, and validation practices, improving critical data quality from below 50% to above 95%.
- **Procurement Analytics:** Led analytics across approximately $14 billion in indirect spend and more than 3,000 suppliers, identifying approximately $10 million in savings and cost avoidance.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Automated reporting processes that reduced refresh times from weeks to under one hour, improving efficiency and reducing manual work.
- **Internal Audit Analytics:** Supported Tyson Foods internal audit engagements through analytical modeling, data validation, and risk-focused insights across supply chain, inventory, procurement, transportation, logistics, and other operational areas.

My academic background includes an MBA with a concentration in Business Analytics, and I am completing an M.S. in Data Science at Bellevue University.

I am particularly interested in applying advanced analytics, machine learning, data engineering, and responsible AI-assisted tools to solve complex business problems.
