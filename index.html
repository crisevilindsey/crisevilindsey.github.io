01 / Introduction

About Me

I am an enterprise analytics leader and hands-on problem solver who believes the most valuable analytics work starts with understanding the business question—not the dashboard.

My career spans analytics strategy, business intelligence, data quality, governance, technology delivery, and applied data science. At Walmart and Tyson Foods, I partnered with business leaders, Finance, operations teams, and technical specialists to turn complex information into practical insights, consistent measures, and scalable analytical solutions.

At Walmart U.S. Learning, within Global People Analytics, I founded and led the Reporting, Data & Analytics function supporting more than one million associates and approximately 200 academies. I hired, coached, and managed four analytics managers; established KPI definitions, reporting standards, and self-service capabilities; and helped leaders understand training participation, assessment results, associate survey feedback, compliance, and operational performance. Our work improved critical data quality from below 50% to above 95%, and analysis supported approximately $4 million in annual travel savings.

In Walmart Global Procurement, I led analytics across approximately $14 billion in indirect spend and more than 3,000 suppliers. Working with Finance and sourcing leaders, I developed reporting and analysis that identified approximately $10 million in savings and cost avoidance. At Tyson Foods, I worked with internal audit and business stakeholders to understand source data and operational processes, build analytical models, and surface risk-focused insights across supply chain, inventory, procurement, transportation, and logistics.

I also enjoy the technical work behind reliable analytics: SQL, data modeling, BI development, data validation, automation, Python, and R. I hold an MBA in Business Analytics and am completing an M.S. in Data Science at Bellevue University. This portfolio brings together my professional leadership experience and academic technical work.
